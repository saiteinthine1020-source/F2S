# F2S Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and leaders pledge to make participation in the F2S community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility, apologising to people affected by mistakes and learning from the experience;
- focusing on what is best for the community and the people F2S is intended to serve; and
- protecting private household, contributor and reporter information.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- sexualised language or imagery, or sexual attention or advances of any kind;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, or personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- pressuring someone to reveal vulnerability, conduct, identity or household information publicly;
- retaliating against a good-faith reporter or reviewer; and
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

Technical disagreement is welcome. Personal attacks, intimidation and careless disclosure are not.

## Enforcement responsibilities

The repository owner and appointed maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, documentation, issues and other contributions that conflict with this Code of Conduct, and may explain moderation decisions when appropriate and safe.

Enforcement decisions should be prompt, fair, proportionate and consistent while protecting reporter privacy and the safety of people involved.

## Scope

This Code applies in all F2S community spaces and when an individual officially represents F2S in public or private spaces. It covers repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, commits, documentation, project communication and related events.

## Reporting conduct concerns

Do not publish sensitive conduct details, private messages, personal information or evidence in a public issue.

For abusive content hosted on GitHub, use GitHub's content-reporting and support tools where available. For a project-level concern:

1. Use a private contact method published on the [repository owner's GitHub profile](https://github.com/saiteinthine1020-source), if available; or
2. open a metadata-only issue titled `Private conduct contact requested` that contains no incident details, names, screenshots or private information; the maintainer will arrange a private channel and close the request.

If the concern involves the repository owner or there is no safe project contact, report applicable GitHub-hosted conduct directly to [GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/). Vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not the conduct-reporting path.

All complaints will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as the available evidence and safety constraints permit. Maintainers must respect the privacy and security of reporters and affected people. Confidentiality cannot be absolute where immediate safety, platform rules or law require escalation, but information will be minimised and shared only as necessary.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers use the following Community Impact Guidelines when determining consequences.

### 1. Correction

**Community impact:** Inappropriate language or other behavior considered unprofessional or unwelcome.

**Consequence:** A private written warning explaining the issue and expected correction. A public clarification or apology may be requested when it can be made safely.

### 2. Warning

**Community impact:** A violation through a single significant incident or a series of actions.

**Consequence:** A warning with consequences for continued behavior and a defined period of no unsolicited interaction with involved people or maintainers. Violating these conditions may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary ban

**Community impact:** A serious violation or sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence:** A temporary ban from interaction or public communication with the community for a specified period. Unsolicited interaction with involved people or enforcement maintainers is prohibited during this period.

### 4. Permanent ban

**Community impact:** A pattern of violations, sustained harassment, aggression, retaliation or disparagement of individuals or groups.

**Consequence:** Permanent exclusion from public interaction within the F2S community.

## Appeals and records

A person subject to enforcement may request one private reconsideration by identifying a factual or proportionality concern. The repository owner or an uninvolved maintainer, when available, reviews the appeal. Repeated contact, harassment or public disclosure is not an appeal.

Moderation records contain only the information needed for consistent enforcement, use restricted access and have an explicit review/retention period. Public explanations omit private details.
